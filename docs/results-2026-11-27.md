## Disk M5 scratch-window A/B

**`OWNER-0105` #2, Disk speed: CLOSED.** Three RAM boots used pinned `dskbench` on the confirmed `linux_rootfs`
scratch window at LBA 64973631 (6,291,456 pages), with the same frozen M5 images that passed 32/32 power-cut
durability on November 26. The owner's Data store was untouched, raw root controls stayed flat, and there were
zero store or UFS kernel errors.

- **M4-conc (`m5bench-m4cc`) — `VERDICT GAIN`**, `rc=0`, zero errors. Across A1 serial → B concurrent → A2 serial,
  4 KiB QD8 read IOPS rose from the serial mean of 13,814 to 61,988, and mixed-reader p99 fell from 2,394.9 to 262.9 µs.
- **M5-wb (`m5bench-m5wb`) — `VERDICT PASS`**, `rc=0`, zero errors. Compared with M4-conc, P1/P2 means delivered
  1.71×–4.12× write throughput, including 3,089.8 MB/s at 1 MiB QD8 and about 11.1k–12.0k IOPS for 4 KiB random
  writes. Sync p50 fell 42%–58%, and mixed-reader p99 fell 7% to 243.7 µs. The provider averaged 99.9% cached
  writes (351,194 of 351,496) and 807 flushes. Cached writes averaged 53.7 µs versus 191.2 µs for FUA on M4-conc.
- **M5-queue (`m5bench-m5q`) — `VERDICT FAIL`**. The inter-phase burst between P1 and P2 hit 401 queue-full
  refusals, with zero in the timed read/write phases. With four read and four write workers, `q_w_us` was 256–289 µs
  across a mean 381,550 queued writes. Versus M4-conc, 4 KiB read IOPS fell to 0.51×–0.57× and 128 KiB write
  throughput to 0.57×, while mixed-reader p99 and `fsync` p50 rose to 1.62× and 1.39×, respectively.

`data-store-write-back=0` and `data-store-queue=0` remain the defaults on `main`. M5-wb is a scratch-qualified daily
candidate pending an owner decision and a fresh owner-store backup. M5-queue is unsuitable for daily use.
