- Chrome played H.264 and HEVC at 1080p and 4K, and VP9 and AV1 at 1080p, with 0–1 dropped frames.
  HEVC went through VideoToolbox.
- The run recorded zero crashes, zero PianoA830 failures and zero sandbox denies.
